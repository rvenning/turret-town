❓ How to Play

  1. Monsters walk the road from the cave to your town. Every one that reaches it costs a ❤️.
  2. Tap a grassy square, then pick a turret. Turrets only shoot what's in their ring.
  3. Tap a turret you own to upgrade it, change what it aims at, or sell it back.
  4. Flyers cut straight across the dotted line — Bomb Lobbers can't reach them.
  5. Armour soaks up a bit of every hit. Watchtowers punch straight through it.
  6. Hit ▶▶ to send the next wave early and pocket the bonus gold. ⏩ speeds the whole battle up.
